Privacy Policy

App: CitizenReady  |  Last updated: September 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how CitizenReady ("the app") handles your information. CitizenReady is developed and maintained by Xhoana Koca.

1. Information We Collect

CitizenReady does not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information. Specifically:

  • We do not require you to create an account.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any contact information.
  • We do not store your quiz results or practice history on any server.
  • All app data (progress, settings) is stored locally on your device only.

2. Microphone and Speech Recognition

CitizenReady includes optional speaking and listening exercises that use your device's microphone and speech recognition. This feature is used only to check your spoken answers in real time. Audio is processed on-device or through your device's built-in speech recognition service (e.g. Android Speech Recognition) and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted to our servers.

3. Advertising

CitizenReady uses Google AdMob to display ads. Google may collect certain data to serve relevant ads, including your device's advertising ID. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. CitizenReady is intended for users 18 and older (the US citizenship exam requires applicants to be adults). We do not direct this app at children and have disabled child-directed ad settings accordingly.

4. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob — advertising
  • Google Speech Recognition — on-device speech processing
  • Expo — app runtime framework

5. Data Retention

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no data retained on our end. Any data stored locally on your device can be cleared by uninstalling the app.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date at the top.
