My name is Xhoana Koca (the "Xh" is pronounced like a "J"). I grew up in Korçë, Albania — curious about technology long before I had reliable access to it. At 12, my family and I moved to the United States, a transition that shaped my resilience and my ability to communicate clearly across cultures and teams.

I graduated Magna Cum Laude from New York City College of Technology (CUNY) with a Bachelor of Technology in Computer Systems Technology — GPA 3.54, Dean's List four semesters. I bridge the gap between technical and business teams: fluent enough in technology to understand system constraints, and clear enough in communication to turn complex requirements into actionable documentation and tested solutions.
